About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for jotting down ideas, reminders and to-dos the moment they come to mind. It keeps everything in one short list so nothing gets lost.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note into the "New note" box on the Home page.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button to save it.
  3. Check your list under "Your notes" to review what you saved.

Features

  • Add notes in one step
  • See a live count of your notes
  • Clean, distraction-free layout
  • Works on desktop and mobile screens

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note you are typing
Tab Move to the next field or button
